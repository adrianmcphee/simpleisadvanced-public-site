How the Logic Is Derived

The method in this book is design derivation. The diagnosis begins with public cases, published research, and recurring problems encountered in practice. The correction is derived by carrying established system-design constraints into the allocation of organisational ownership and authority. The transfer rests on the same structural relation: somebody must own state, boundaries must contain meaningful change, interactions need explicit meaning, consequential action needs authority, and claims need identities and evidence.

Software engineering has accumulated these constraints because their absence produces predictable failure. Shared mutable state creates contention. An undefined interface has to be renegotiated. A change without provenance cannot be reproduced. A temporary exception without retirement becomes architecture. A company that refuses those conditions in production can still reproduce every one of them in its organisation, then hire people to reconcile the result socially.

The human institution changes the design

A company is not a distributed system. People have rights, judgement, memory, incentives, relationships, and occasionally lawyers. Law may reserve a decision, employment may require consultation or consent, and independent challenge may matter precisely because a process owner should not mark their own homework. Power is contested rather than configured.

Those differences do not make the system constraints disappear. They determine how the organisational analogue must be designed. A legitimate divided right names the harm it prevents, its independent holder, its threshold, its evidence, and its return path. A unit remains accountable for the complete outcome without acquiring powers that law, governance, or human dignity place elsewhere. Chapter 10 develops that division of authority; Chapter 11 shows how bounded authority reaches production.

The derivation earns its value through observable movement: shorter decision time, fewer recurring coordination hours, more reproducible production claims, lower failure and rework cost, and less unit time consumed by central obligations that do not change the outcome. Chapter 13 attaches those predictions to an economic test. If a boundary performs badly, the boundary changes. The vocabulary is allowed to leave with it.
