Sources and Evidential Limits

This note states the book's evidence contract, identifies the public records behind its principal factual anchors, and describes the limits attached to them. The verification cut-off is 22 August 2026. Documented incidents show that particular failure mechanisms and consequences occurred. Published research tests narrower relationships. Any complete operating model built from this logic remains a synthesis to test.

The evidence contract

The book uses four kinds of material.

Documented cases and research are named in the text. Knight Capital, the Post Office Horizon scandal, DORA's delivery research, the Windows Vista study, and the Harvard work on organisational mirroring support specific claims about failure, delivery, and the relationship between organisational and technical structure. They do not prove the complete operating model.

Composite cases show recurring mechanisms that are difficult to describe without people, decisions, and consequences. They combine observations from more than one organisation and are marked Composite and set in italics. The organisations, characters, sequence, and numerical details do not describe a documented engagement. Confidential work informed which patterns seemed worth examining, but a reader cannot inspect that work and should give it no independent evidential weight.

Worked examples test the internal logic of a method with invented inputs. They are marked Worked example and remain in roman type. Their numbers are assumptions, not measured outcomes. Familiarity is not validation. A composite that resembles your employer may identify a useful hypothesis; it does not establish its truth.

First-person observations describe work the author directly experienced and are marked Author observation. The startup operating arrangement in Chapter 4 and the job-specification episode in Chapter 10 are the two instances. Because the organisations are unnamed, readers cannot verify the details independently. These observations show that an arrangement or artefact existed and explain why a mechanism is worth testing. They do not establish prevalence, causation, or a general performance claim.

The strongest fair objection is therefore correct: the public evidence establishes recurring failure modes and several component mechanisms, not the universal superiority of this logic or of any exact design built from it. A complete implementation is a synthesis and a testable hypothesis. The argument should be judged by whether its causal chain is coherent, whether each authority and control can be implemented, and whether a bounded field test produces better outcomes than the structure it replaces.

How to test the model

Choose one consequential process with a measurable customer or operator outcome. Record its present performance, cost, failure distribution, coordination demand, and control record before changing the structure. Define the process and its promise. Give one unit the authority described in this book. Preserve the constraints that matter. Predeclare the leading indicators, lagging outcomes, guardrails, and conditions that would count against the model.

Run the unit long enough to observe operation rather than launch energy. Compare the result with its own baseline and, where possible, a similar process that retained the existing structure. Record governance interventions, contract failures, supplier incidents, escaped defects, and coordination that returned through another name. Do not move the baseline, recategorise failures, or call activity an outcome.

If the unit cannot reduce elapsed decision time, improve the promised outcome, contain risk, and make its state more legible without recreating a coordination layer, the model has failed that test. Modify or reject it. A methodology that cannot describe evidence against itself is another member of the Alignment-Industrial Complex.

Current technical limits

No single product currently performs the complete reconciliation system described here. Code and document analysis, schema validation, provenance, and bounded comparison between a defined process and its implementation are available now with human review. Continuous cross-artefact Drift detection requires engineering. Decision-to-outcome tracing and fully automated synthesis across a corporation are not yet dependable. The owned, versioned artefact is durable; models and vendors are replaceable.

Documented cases

Knight Capital. Chapter 5 cites a pre-tax loss of approximately US\$440 million over forty-five minutes, with new code deployed to seven of eight servers and dormant “Power Peg” code activated on the eighth. The US\$440 million figure is Knight Capital Group's Form 8-K of 2 August 2012. The deployment sequence, more than four million executions, and a loss above US\$460 million appear in the US Securities and Exchange Commission's administrative order, In the Matter of Knight Capital Americas LLC, 16 October 2013. The body uses the company's own loss figure; the SEC figure differs. The four-million figure counts executions, not orders.

TSB. Chapter 11 compares TSB's gross post-migration charges with its later regulatory penalty. TSB Banking Group's 2018 Annual Report and Accounts disclosed £330.2 million in post-migration charges. The Financial Conduct Authority and Prudential Regulation Authority Final Notices of 20 December 2022 imposed penalties of £29.75 million and £18.9 million. TSB later recognised a partial recovery against the gross charge. The comparison shows the scale of total consequence relative to a visible fine; it does not estimate what a different investment would have prevented.

Birmingham City Council. Chapter 11 uses Grant Thornton's Report in the Public Interest concerning the implementation of the Oracle ERP system, 11 February 2025, for the original £19 million business case, governance and testing findings, and consequences for accounts and fraud control. The council's written answer of 27 January 2026 forecast £144.4 million of expenditure through 2027–28. The University of Sheffield Audit Reform Lab's August 2024 analysis estimated a wider £216.5 million impact including written-off savings. These measures answer different questions and should not be interchanged. The council's September 2023 section 114 notice was driven primarily by equal-pay liabilities; the ERP failure was a serious contributing problem rather than the sole cause. A UK council cannot legally become insolvent, which is why “effective bankruptcy” requires care.

Post Office Horizon. The conclusion refers to the Post Office Horizon IT Inquiry as a public reconstruction of records, decisions, and consequences. Established in 2020 and placed on a statutory footing on 1 June 2021, the inquiry held public hearings from 2022 to 2024. Volume 1 of its final report, published 8 July 2025, addresses human impact and redress. On 8 July 2026, the Chair confirmed that the remainder of the report was still in preparation. This book does not attribute unpublished findings to the inquiry.

Regulatory anchors

UK individual accountability. Chapter 10 uses the Financial Conduct Authority's Senior Managers Regime guidance and FCA Handbook provisions SUP 10C and SYSC 26. They require statements of responsibility for Senior Management Functions and systematic allocation of overall responsibility across a firm's activities, subject to the regime applicable to the firm. The chapter argues that explicit process ownership and evidence can support those duties. It does not prescribe a legal allocation, displace the accountable senior manager, or claim that one unit design satisfies every firm's reasonable-steps obligation.

Model risk. Chapter 10 uses the April 2026 version of the Prudential Regulation Authority's supervisory statement SS1/23, Model risk management principles for banks, including its expectations for board and senior-management accountability, model inventory and tiering, development and use, independent validation, governance, and model-risk mitigants. The PRA applies the principles proportionately. The chapter's allocation among unit ownership, second-line challenge, and independent assurance is an operating-model proposal inside those expectations; it is not an official interpretation that every AI system has the same regulatory classification.

Digital operational resilience. Chapter 10 uses Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, the Digital Operational Resilience Act. Article 5 assigns the management body responsibility for the ICT risk-management framework and ultimate responsibility for ICT risk; the Regulation also requires oversight of ICT third-party arrangements. The book uses those provisions to reject the idea that a technical integration transfers process ownership. Exact governance, notification, contractual, and oversight duties depend on the entity, service, criticality, and applicable law.

Published research

DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA). Chapter 4 cites the 2019 Accelerate State of DevOps report's category comparison: elite performers deployed 208 times more frequently than low performers and had lead times 106 times faster. Those multiples are derived from survey-response categories and vary across years; they are not constants or a prediction for a particular company. Chapter 11 cites the 2024 report's modelled association: each 25% increase in AI adoption corresponded to an estimated 1.5% reduction in delivery throughput and 7.2% reduction in delivery stability. The 2025 State of AI-assisted Software Development report found higher AI adoption associated with greater throughput and greater instability and described AI as an amplifier of the surrounding system. DORA is a Google Cloud research programme and the results are survey-based associations.

Organisational structure and software quality. Chapter 4 cites Nachiappan Nagappan, Brendan Murphy, and Victor Basili, “The Influence of Organizational Structure on Software Quality: An Empirical Case Study,” ICSE 2008. On Windows Vista, their organisational model reported approximately 86% precision and 84% recall and outperformed the code metrics tested in that study. It also cites Alan MacCormack, Carliss Baldwin, and John Rusnak, “Exploring the Duality between Product and Organizational Architectures: A Test of the Mirroring Hypothesis,” Research Policy, 2012. The papers support a relationship between organisational and technical structure. They do not establish that every reorganisation produces a predictable architecture.

Cost of poor software quality. Chapter 11 cites the Consortium for Information & Software Quality, The Cost of Poor Software Quality in the US: A 2022 Report. Its macroeconomic model estimates US\$2.41 trillion in total cost and approximately US\$1.52 trillion in accumulated technical debt. The report was co-sponsored by Synopsys. These modelled national estimates do not provide an observed cost ledger or a benchmark for a company investment case.

What would count against the argument

The operating model would be weakened if process-owning units with explicit authority, contracts, baselines, and reconciliation showed no durable improvement over comparable processes governed through functional coordination. It would be weakened if the removed coordination cost reappeared in equal or greater measure inside contracts, governance, orchestration, or duplicated capability. It would be weakened if local autonomy produced uncontrolled institutional risk that explicit policy, audit, and escalation could not contain. It would be weakened if the AI reader created enough false confidence, review burden, or artefact gaming to exceed the reconciliation work it replaced.

Those conditions are measurable. The book therefore asks for one bounded test, not belief in a transformation narrative.
