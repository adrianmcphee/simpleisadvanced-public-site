The End of Alignment

The AI-Era Operating Model for Software-Dependent Corporates

For leaders, operators, product authorities and engineers

Read the online edition

Software-dependent corporates separated operations, product, engineering, data, risk and finance, then built an alignment economy to stitch them back together. AI makes the cost visible and the delay untenable.

The End of Alignment explains the replacement: process-owning autonomous units, explicit contracts, milestones, lanes, leases, trunk integration, controlled exposure and continuous reconciliation. It is written for leaders who want to change how work actually ships.
